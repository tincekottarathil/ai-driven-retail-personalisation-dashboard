# AI-Driven Personalisation in Retail – Power BI Dashboard

## Overview

This project uses Microsoft Power BI to analyse how AI-powered product recommendations relate to customer engagement and purchasing behaviour in retail.

The dashboard focuses on the customer perspective, exploring how recommendation types and device types relate to clicks, add-to-cart actions, and purchases.

## Business Questions

The analysis addresses three questions:

1. Which type of AI recommendation receives the highest customer click rate?
2. How does customer engagement with AI recommendations vary across different device types?
3. How does customer engagement with AI recommendations relate to purchase rate?

## Key Findings

- **Recommendation performance:** Personalised recommendations recorded the highest click rate at **67.0%**.
- **Device engagement:** Mobile users recorded the highest click rate at **61.4%**.
- **Purchase behaviour:** Interactions where customers clicked a recommendation had a purchase rate of **76.3%**, compared with **36.3%** for interactions where they did not click.

These findings show differences in engagement and purchase rates across the categories analysed. They describe associations in the dataset and do not prove that clicking a recommendation causes a purchase.

## Dashboard Features

The Power BI dashboard includes:

- KPI cards for total interactions, total clicks, click rate, add-to-cart rate, and purchase rate.
- Customer click rate by AI recommendation type.
- Customer engagement by device type, including click and add-to-cart rates.
- Purchase rate comparison between clicked and not-clicked recommendations.
- A key insights section summarising the main findings.

## Dataset

The project uses the **Retail AI Recommendation & Personalization Dataset** by Noopur Bhatt, available on Kaggle.

- **Source:** [Retail AI Recommendation & Personalization Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/noopurbhatt/retail-ai-recommendation-and-personalization-dataset)
- **Records:** 250,000 interactions
- **Columns:** 31

The analysis uses fields relating to recommendation type, device type, clicks, add-to-cart actions, and purchases.
